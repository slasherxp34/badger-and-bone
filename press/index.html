The brand, in two paragraphs

Badger & Bone was founded in September 2026 in Los Angeles by a first-time founder, and makes gifts and home goods for one specific dog. There is no decade of heritage behind it and we are not going to invent one. What there is: an original illustration system — original drawings from our own six-coat system, six dachshund coats in three poses, created for this brand and nobody else. The drawings are built as code in our own drawing system, with AI-assisted tooling used to write and refine that code rather than to generate the images. There is no named illustrator because there is no illustrator to name, and we would rather say that than invent a studio. No stock illustration, no clip art, no traced artwork. The construction is published in full, including the source files, so the claim is checkable rather than asserted.

Everything is printed to order in the USA by US print partners and shipped direct — nothing sits in a warehouse, and nothing is forwarded from an overseas marketplace. Four products take a customer-typed name, printed exactly as it is entered. The product list is deliberately short, the prices and delivery dates are published before anyone pays, and there are no fake reviews, no countdown timers and no permanent struck-through sale price. It is a small, honest operation, and the parts of it that are not finished yet are named on the site rather than papered over.

Why long dogs need long things

The whole brand runs on one proportion. A dachshund is drawn — and the products are cut — three times longer nose to tail than the dog is tall at the shoulder. That 3:1 rule is treated as a specification rather than a joke, and it is the reason the puzzle is a landscape composition instead of a tidy square, the art prints run wide rather than tall, and the mug gives up having a front at all — a dachshund does not fit on one face of a cylinder, so the drawing wraps the whole way round. Almost everything made for dogs is made for a dog shaped like a Labrador; a dachshund owner has spent years buying things that are the wrong shape for the animal. That is the gap, and the 3:1 rule is what closes it. It is also the honest reason there are only 13 products: each one has to earn the shape.

Creators and gifting

We gift product to dachshund creators. That is the whole program, stated plainly, because a vague one wastes your time and ours.

What we offer

  • Gifted product, free and yours to keep. Usually two items, chosen with you. It is a gift — you owe us nothing.
  • No post required. Take the product, post nothing, and that is a completely fine outcome. We would rather gift ten and see three posts than ask ten people for a favor.
  • No approval rights over what you say. If the product is not good, say so. We are new and we need the truth more than we need a nice post.
  • Nothing is conditional on you being positive. Not the gift, not a second one, not anything. Conditioning a gift on sentiment is prohibited under the FTC Reviews Rule (§465.4), and it is also just a bad way to behave.

What we need back, and it is only one thing

If you do choose to post, the gift has to be disclosed. A free product is a material connection under the FTC’s Endorsement Guides, and the disclosure has to be clear, conspicuous and early — near the start of the caption, before the “more” cut, not buried in a hashtag wall at the bottom.

The exact line, which is what we ask for in every outreach email: “Gifted by @badgerandbone”. “#ad” also works. Please don’t rely on “thanks”, “collab”, “sp” or “partner” — the FTC has said those are not enough on their own, and the brand is liable too, so this is the one thing we will follow up about.

What we do not offer, yet

No pay-per-post, no rate card, no affiliate program. Our budget for paid placement is zero and we are not going to pretend otherwise. If you have a rate card, we genuinely cannot meet it right now — a polite no costs us nothing and we would rather you knew before replying. If a paid program starts later, the people we gifted to first are the people we will email first.

Interested? Email hello@badgerandbone.com with your account and your dog’s name. There is no form and no application.

Rescues and shelters

Straight answer first: we do not run a donation program yet. There is no percentage of any sale going anywhere, and you will not find that claim on this site, because making it before the paperwork exists would be both a legal problem and a lie. When there is something real to announce, it will be announced with the partner named and the terms written down.

What we can do today, and have done: donate product for a raffle, an auction or an adoption-day table. It costs a rescue nothing, it needs no contract, and it is the version of this that is honest on day one.

If you are a Southern California dachshund rescue — foster-based or with a facility, 501(c)(3) or not — email hello@badgerandbone.com and tell us what you have coming up. We are in Los Angeles and can hand things over in person. We are also happy to be told no.

One thing we will not do: we are not affiliated with any kennel club, breed registry or breed association, and nothing on this site is endorsed or sanctioned by one. “Dachshund” and “long dog” are used descriptively and nothing more.

Facts we can’t confirm yet

A press page is where a new business is most tempted to fill a gap with something plausible. These are the gaps, named:

  • The founder’s name and photo. Not published yet — a first-time founder in Los Angeles, and we would rather introduce ourselves holding the actual product than put up a stock headshot. If you need a name for a byline or a credit line, email and ask; it is not a secret, it is just not on the site until the samples land.
  • Product photographs. There are none. Every image on this site, including every file in the kit above, is an original render or drawing — the first samples are on their way, and photographing a product we have not held would mean using a supplier mock-up and calling it ours. Real photographs go up beside the drawings as soon as they exist.
  • Customer reviews and sales numbers. We have neither to quote. The store is new, every product page says “be the first to review this product”, and we do not write, buy or import reviews. Any figure you see attributed to us that is not on this page did not come from us.

Anything above that changes will change here first. If a detail matters for something you are writing, ask — hello@badgerandbone.com — and you will get a straight answer, including “we don’t know yet”.
